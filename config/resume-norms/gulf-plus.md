# Resume Norms: Gulf+ (Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain)
Last updated: 2026-05-29
Source: Saudi HRDF/MODON practices, GulfTalent recruiter conventions, Bayt Saudi/Qatar data

## What Gulf+ covers

This is an extension of the UAE campaign targeting neighbouring Gulf markets:
- **Saudi Arabia**: SDAIA, Saudi Aramco Digital, STC, Tonomus (NEOM), stc pay, PIF portfolio companies
- **Qatar**: QatarEnergy Digital, Ooredoo, Qatar Foundation, Qatar Airways AI
- **Kuwait / Bahrain**: Smaller markets, volume play for Tier 3

The resume norms are nearly identical to UAE with a few country-specific adjustments per below.

## Mandatory inclusions (same as UAE plus these)

- **Nationality**: Include. Saudi/Qatar employers factor this into visa cost models. "Indian National" is common and not a blocker for tech roles.
- **Relocation statement**: "Available to relocate to [Saudi Arabia / Qatar] immediately upon offer."
- **Visa openness**: "Open to [Saudi Iqama / Qatar Residence Permit] sponsorship."
- **Phone**: +91 India number. WhatsApp is used in Gulf.
- **LinkedIn URL**: Critical.

## Saudi-specific additions

- **Saudi Vision 2030 alignment**: For Saudi roles, mention AI relevance to Vision 2030 in the cover letter (not resume): "production agentic AI systems aligning with Saudi Vision 2030 digital transformation priorities."
- **Saudization (Nitaqat) awareness**: Some roles require a Saudi national co-hire. This rarely affects AI specialist roles at international companies or Saudi tech entities, but be aware of it.
- **SDAIA (Saudi Data & AI Authority)**: Highest priority Saudi employer. Treat like UAE Tier 1.

## Qatar-specific additions

- **Qatar National Vision 2030**: Similar to Saudi Vision 2030. Reference in cover letters for Qatari employers.
- **QatarEnergy**: Major employer. AI/Digital transformation roles are growing significantly.

## Prohibited / omit (same as UAE)

- **Photo**: Omit for ATS submissions. Acceptable on direct applications to some Gulf companies.
- **Religion**: Never include.
- **Salary expectation**: Do not include on resume.

## Length

- **2 pages** — same as UAE.

## Work authorization statement

For Saudi roles:
> "Available to relocate to Saudi Arabia immediately; open to Saudi work visa (Iqama) sponsorship."

For Qatar roles:
> "Available to relocate to Qatar immediately; open to Qatar residence permit sponsorship."

Generic Gulf+:
> "Available to relocate across GCC; open to work visa sponsorship."

## Format

- **Paper**: A4
- **Language**: English for all AI/tech roles in Gulf+ — Arabic not required
- **Currency**: SAR (Saudi Riyal) or QAR (Qatari Riyal) for comp discussions. Don't put on resume.

## ATS / portals

- **Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby**: Increasingly used by Saudi tech companies (STC, stc pay, Tamara). Scannable via scan.mjs.
- **Bayt.com (Saudi/Qatar sections)**: Primary Gulf job board with strong Saudi/Qatar presence.
- **GulfTalent**: Covers the entire GCC. Good AI/tech section.
- **Indeed Saudi / Indeed Qatar**: Growing presence; use for volume scan.
- **LinkedIn Saudi Arabia / Qatar**: Active but use manual apply.
- **NaukriGulf**: Covers Saudi and Qatar as well as UAE.

## Summary framing

Same as UAE but replace relocation destination:

For Saudi:
```
... Available to relocate to Saudi Arabia immediately; open to Saudi work visa sponsorship.
```

For Qatar:
```
... Available to relocate to Qatar immediately; open to Qatar residence permit sponsorship.
```

## Red flags to avoid

- Do NOT include Mistral AI (Saudi office) — Lever portal has aggressive hCaptcha (blocked)
- Do NOT confuse UAE rules with Saudi rules — Saudi Saudization quotas affect some roles
- Do NOT ignore SDAIA and Tonomus/NEOM — these are the Saudi equivalents of UAE's G42/Core42
- Do NOT apply to roles explicitly requiring Saudi/Qatari nationality
