# Resume Norms: UAE
Last updated: 2026-05-29
Source: UAE tech hiring practice, Bayt/NaukriGulf conventions, recruiter feedback

## Mandatory inclusions

- **Nationality**: Include. UAE employers routinely filter by nationality for visa cost estimation. "Indian National" is not a blocker in tech — state it plainly.
- **Current location**: "Hyderabad, India" (or wherever Amarsh is based)
- **Relocation statement**: Must appear in summary or header. Preferred phrasing: "Available to relocate to UAE immediately upon offer."
- **Visa sponsorship openness**: In summary — "Open to UAE Employment Visa sponsorship." Do NOT say "requires sponsorship" (sounds demanding) — say "open to sponsorship."
- **LinkedIn URL**: Required. UAE recruiters verify LinkedIn before calling.
- **GitHub URL**: Include for technical roles.
- **Phone**: Include with India country code (+91). UAE recruiters call WhatsApp.

## Prohibited / omit

- **Photo**: Omit for ATS-submitted resumes. Acceptable on direct email applications to local boutique agencies, but default to omitting.
- **Religion / marital status**: Never include unless explicitly asked.
- **Salary expectation**: Do not put on resume. Discuss verbally.
- **"References available on request"**: Filler — omit.

## Length

- **2 pages** is the UAE tech standard for senior roles (5+ years experience).
- 1 page is acceptable only for early-career.
- 3 pages is acceptable for 10+ year profiles but risky with ATS — prefer 2.
- Amarsh: target **2 pages**.

## Photo

- **Default: omit.** Most UAE tech job portals (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever used by international companies in UAE) are photo-blind.
- Exception: if applying directly to a UAE national company (ADNOC, du, Etisalat) via their own portal and they explicitly request a photo, include a professional headshot.

## Work authorization statement

Add to Professional Summary, last sentence:

> "Available to relocate to Dubai / Abu Dhabi immediately. Open to UAE Employment Visa sponsorship."

Alternatively in the header line under name/contact:

> Location: Hyderabad, India → Targeting Dubai / Abu Dhabi | Open to UAE Employment Visa

## Format

- **Paper**: A4 (UAE standard)
- **Font**: Clean professional sans-serif (existing Space Grotesk / DM Sans template is fine)
- **Email format**: PDF strongly preferred; DOCX acceptable for email applications

## ATS / portals

- **Bayt**: Uploads PDF; ATS parses plain text sections well. Avoid tables in PDF.
- **NaukriGulf**: Similar to Naukri India — plain text sections parse best. Profile data entered separately anyway.
- **Greenhouse / Ashby / Lever**: Used by international companies in UAE (G42, Core42, Presight, MBZUAI). Standard ATS, PDF is fine.
- **LinkedIn Easy Apply**: On hold (fills context with logs). Use direct portal where available.
- **Taleo / Workday**: Some large UAE banks use these. Paste plain text in addition to PDF upload.

## Summary framing

Open with production impact + agentic AI specialisation, close with UAE relocation readiness:

```
Senior AI Engineer with 6+ years building production agentic systems, LLM platforms, and enterprise GenAI automation.
Currently at OpenText (India) delivering AI Cockpit (multi-agent planning + test automation, 10x faster delivery) and
a LangGraph-based CI/CD migration agent (12x acceleration, hackathon winner). Specialised in RAG, multi-agent orchestration,
MCP tooling, and LLMOps. Available to relocate to Dubai / Abu Dhabi immediately; open to UAE Employment Visa sponsorship.
```

Key UAE market phrases to inject where authentic:
- "production-ready agentic systems"
- "enterprise GenAI"
- "LLM orchestration"
- "UAE Vision 2031 AI priorities" (only in cover letter, not resume)

## Red flags to avoid

- Do NOT frame as "looking to leave current role due to..." — UAE recruiters value stability narrative
- Do NOT list salary expectations on resume
- Do NOT say "currently based in India" without pairing it with "relocating to UAE" — standalone India mention reads as low-commitment
- Do NOT use US Letter format (8.5×11) — submit A4
- Do NOT include a 3-line address block — one line city/country is enough
