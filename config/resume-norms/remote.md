# Resume Norms: Worldwide Remote
Last updated: 2026-05-29
Source: Remote-first hiring practice, contractor platforms (Arc.dev, Toptal, Deel), global remote job boards

## What "Worldwide Remote" means for this campaign

A role is a worldwide remote target if:
- The JD explicitly says "remote", "fully remote", "remote worldwide", "remote - international", or "work from anywhere"
- The company uses a global EOR (Employer of Record) like Deel, Remote.com, Rippling — which allows legal employment of international contractors
- The company hires as independent contractors / C2C / 1099 internationally

Do NOT apply to roles that say "remote" but require being in a specific country ("remote in the US", "remote - EU only") unless that country's norms apply and you meet them. Use the relevant country's norms file for those.

## Mandatory inclusions

- **"Remote-ready" signal**: Explicitly state remote availability. Best placed in contact line:
  > Location: Hyderabad, India (IST, UTC+5:30) | Remote-First
- **Contractor / employment framing**: State how you can be legally engaged:
  > "Available as a full-time remote employee via EOR (Deel/Remote.com) or as an independent contractor on C2C basis."
- **Time zone**: IST (UTC+5:30). Many remote companies care about overlap windows. Note: "6h overlap with CET, 9.5h overlap with EST mornings."
- **Async-first credentials**: Mention async collaboration experience if genuine. OpenText's distributed team structure qualifies.
- **LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio links**: Critical for remote — no in-person impression; your digital footprint is your first impression.

## Prohibited / omit

- **Photo**: Never.
- **Region-specific visa statements**: Remove UAE relocation, SGP EP, UK Skilled Worker framing. Replace with the contractor/EOR framing above.
- **Location-specific compensation targets**: Do not specify AED/SGD/GBP salary targets. Remote roles often negotiate in USD; let the discussion happen in the call.
- **Any statement that implies you need relocation support**: Remote roles don't offer relocation. Be clear you're applying remote-only.

## Length

- **2 pages** universal standard for remote senior tech roles.

## Work authorization / engagement statement

Header contact line example:
```
Amarsh Pedapati | AI Engineer
Hyderabad, India (UTC+5:30) | Remote-First
amarsh@email.com | linkedin.com/in/amarshp | github.com/amarshp
Available: EOR employment (Deel/Remote.com) or C2C contractor
```

In summary (last sentence):
> "Available for fully remote engagement worldwide — EOR employment or independent contractor; UTC+5:30, 6h overlap with EU mornings, 9.5h overlap with US East morning."

## Format

- **Paper**: A4 (or Letter if the company is US-based — detect from the job description)
- **Language**: English universally

## ATS / portals

- **Greenhouse / Ashby / Lever**: Most remote-first tech companies use these. Highest priority — scan.mjs can directly query these APIs.
- **Arc.dev**: Remote-focused developer platform, supports international contractors. Strong AI engineer listings.
- **Himalayas.app**: High-quality remote job board with strong AI/ML section. Many companies post Greenhouse/Lever links here.
- **Wellfound (AngelList)**: US startup-centric, many remote. Strong for AI-first startups.
- **WeWorkRemotely.com**: Curated remote roles. Good AI/ML section.
- **Remotive.com**: Remote-only board with good tech curation.
- **Remote.co**: Vetted remote companies list.
- **Toptal / Gun.io**: Contractor marketplaces. Better for contract/project work than full-time.

## Summary framing

Remove all country-specific relocation language. Lead with production AI credentials:

```
Senior AI Engineer with 6+ years delivering production agentic systems, LLM platforms, and enterprise
GenAI automation in distributed team environments. At OpenText: AI Cockpit (multi-agent orchestration,
10x delivery acceleration, LangSmith tracing, deployed to enterprise clients) and LangGraph self-healing
CI/CD migration agent (12x acceleration, adopted by 10+ global teams). Deep expertise in agentic AI,
RAG pipelines, MCP tooling, and LLMOps. Available for fully remote roles worldwide — UTC+5:30,
strong async collaboration track record; open to EOR employment or C2C contractor arrangements.
```

Key remote-market phrases:
- "distributed team environments" — signals async experience
- "globally adopted" for projects used across teams
- "async-first" workflow mentions
- "documentation-driven development" — remote companies value this
- For US-origin remote companies: use C2C/1099 framing, not relocation language

## Red flags to avoid

- Do NOT include region-specific visa statements (no "UAE Employment Visa", "Skilled Worker visa")
- Do NOT say "open to relocation" — it contradicts applying for a remote role
- Do NOT omit the time zone — remote hiring managers always ask; put it on the resume
- Do NOT use vague "remote-friendly" language — be explicit about EOR vs contractor capability
- Do NOT apply if the JD says "remote in [specific country]" without checking that country's norms
