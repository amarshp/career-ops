# Resume Norms: Australia
Last updated: 2026-05-29
Source: Australian Home Affairs Skills in Demand visa guidelines, Seek recruiter practice, CSOL 2025

## Mandatory inclusions

- **Visa sponsorship note**: "Require Skills in Demand (SID) visa sponsorship (Subclass 482 — Specialist Skills stream)."
  - Use "Specialist Skills stream" framing — this signals you know you meet the AUD 141,210+ salary threshold, which removes the occupation-list constraint and speeds processing to 7–30 days.
- **Current location + relocation intent**: "Bengaluru, India — open to relocation to Sydney / Melbourne / Australia."
- **LinkedIn URL**: Critical — Australian recruiters use LinkedIn extensively.
- **GitHub URL**: Important for AI/ML roles at Atlassian, Canva, tech companies.

## Prohibited / omit

- **Photo**: Never. Not Australian practice; including one raises red flags.
- **Date of birth**: Never.
- **Marital status / religion**: Never.
- **Salary expectation**: Do not include. Salary discussion happens after the technical screen in Australia.

## Length

- **2 pages** is Australian standard for senior professionals.
- 3 pages is acceptable for 10+ year profiles, but 2 pages is cleaner.
- Amarsh: **2 pages**.

## Photo

- **Never include.** See prohibited section.

## Work authorization statement

Add to contact line or end of summary:

> "Require Skills in Demand (Subclass 482) visa sponsorship — Specialist Skills stream."

The Specialist Skills framing is strategic: it signals you earn above AUD 141,210, which means no occupation list restriction and fast processing (7–30 days vs 8 months for Core Skills).

## Format

- **Paper**: A4 (Australia uses A4, not US Letter)
- **Australian English**: Use UK/Australian spellings (organised, specialised, colour)
- **Date format**: DD/MM/YYYY or Month YYYY

## ATS / portals

- **Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby**: Atlassian, Canva, Afterpay/Block, Xero use these. Scannable via scan.mjs.
- **Seek.com.au**: Australia's dominant job board. AI roles listed here. Essential to monitor.
- **LinkedIn AU**: Very active for tech roles. Manual apply.
- **Indeed AU**: Lower signal but reasonable volume.
- **WorkforAustralia**: Government portal, less relevant for AI specialist roles.

## Summary framing

Lead with impact, close with SID visa framing:

```
Senior AI Engineer with 6+ years delivering production agentic systems, LLM platforms, and enterprise
GenAI automation. At OpenText: AI Cockpit (multi-agent orchestration, 10x delivery acceleration,
expert agents with RBAC and structured-output validation) and LangGraph self-healing CI/CD migration
agent (12x acceleration, hackathon winner, adopted by 10+ teams). Deep expertise in agentic AI, RAG
pipelines, MCP tooling, and LLMOps. Open to relocation to Australia; require Skills in Demand
(Subclass 482 Specialist Skills) visa sponsorship.
```

Key AU market phrases:
- "production systems at scale" — Atlassian, Canva, Xero think at scale
- "measurable impact" — Australian tech culture is metrics-driven like the US
- Fintech AI signal: Afterpay/Block, ANZ, Commonwealth Bank, Westpac all building AI
- "open-source" contributions are valued in the AU developer community

## Path to PR note (useful for cover letter, not resume)

SID visa → Employer Nomination Scheme (Subclass 186) after 2 years of sponsored employment → Permanent Residence. This is a direct path, no points game. Worth mentioning in interviews as a signal of long-term commitment.

## Red flags to avoid

- Do NOT include a photo
- Do NOT use US Letter format
- Do NOT say "need visa" generically — be specific: "Require Subclass 482 Specialist Skills sponsorship"
- Do NOT omit quantified achievements — Australian hiring managers, like US counterparts, expect numbers on every significant bullet
- Do NOT use UK English spellings inconsistently (e.g., mixing "optimized" and "organised")
