# Resume Norms: United Kingdom
Last updated: 2026-05-29
Source: UK Home Office Skilled Worker guidance, London AI recruiter practice, IT Jobs Watch data

## Mandatory inclusions

- **Right to work statement**: Must appear — either at the bottom of contact line or final sentence of summary.
  For Amarsh: "Require Skilled Worker visa sponsorship to work in the UK."
- **LinkedIn URL**: UK tech recruiters always check LinkedIn first.
- **GitHub URL**: Non-negotiable for AI/LLM roles — UK AI-native hiring managers will look.
- **Current location**: "Bengaluru, India — open to relocation to London / UK."
- **Portfolio/project links**: UK AI-native startups value demonstrable work (DevOps Copilot Plugin, PersonaRAG).

## Prohibited / omit

- **Photo**: Never. Not UK hiring practice; including one signals unfamiliarity with UK norms.
- **Date of birth / age**: Never.
- **Marital status / religion**: Never.
- **Salary expectation**: Do not include on resume.
- **Full address**: City/country only — "Bengaluru, India."

## Length

- **2 pages** is standard for senior UK tech roles (5+ years experience).
- 3 pages is occasionally acceptable for Staff/Principal (8+ years) — avoid it.
- Amarsh: **tight 2 pages**.

## Photo

- **Never include.** See prohibited section.

## Work authorization statement

Add as final sentence of summary OR as a footnote line below contact info:

> "Require UK Skilled Worker visa sponsorship."

OR combined with location:
> Location: Bengaluru, India → Targeting London | Require Skilled Worker Visa Sponsorship

**Do NOT say "need visa" or "need work permit"** — Skilled Worker is the correct UK terminology.
Employers with a Home Office sponsor licence can sponsor this; most UK tech companies do.

## Format

- **Paper**: A4
- **UK English**: use British spellings (organised, specialised, colour) — or consistent US if the company is US-origin
- **Date format**: DD/MM/YYYY in correspondence; month-name format (May 2026) on resume is cleaner

## ATS / portals

- **Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby**: Used by virtually all UK AI-native companies (Anthropic UK, Google DeepMind, Cohere, ElevenLabs, Wayve, Synthesia). Standard PDF.
- **Workday**: Large UK enterprises (HSBC, Barclays, BT). Paste into form AND upload PDF.
- **Otta.com**: UK-focused job board for senior tech roles. Good AI signal.
- **Cord.co**: UK tech networking + jobs. Less volume, higher signal.
- **TotalJobs / Reed**: High volume UK boards. Less signal for AI specialist roles.
- **LinkedIn**: Significant UK presence but blocked for automated use. Flag for manual.

## Summary framing

Lead with production AI impact, close with UK relocation + sponsorship:

```
Senior AI Engineer with 6+ years delivering production agentic systems, LLM platforms, and enterprise
GenAI automation. At OpenText: AI Cockpit (multi-agent orchestration, 10x delivery acceleration, RBAC,
structured-output validation via FastAPI + LangSmith) and LangGraph self-healing CI/CD migration agent
(12x acceleration, hackathon winner, 10+ teams). Specialised in agentic AI, RAG, MCP tooling, and LLMOps.
Open to relocation to the UK; require Skilled Worker visa sponsorship.
```

Key UK market phrases:
- "production-grade" — strong signal in UK AI-native circles
- "LangGraph" and "MCP tooling" — niche but increasingly valued by UK AI startups
- "evaluation pipeline" — UK AI companies (DeepMind, Cohere) are evaluation-obsessed
- "stakeholder-facing" — UK enterprise values this framing

## London premium

If applying specifically to London roles, note: London pays 28% more than the rest of the UK for AI engineers. Always clarify "open to London-based role" if the job is London. Don't default to "remote only" for UK applications unless the role is explicitly remote-first.

## Red flags to avoid

- Do NOT include a photo
- Do NOT use US Letter format
- Do NOT say "I-9 eligible" or any US immigration term
- Do NOT write "References available on request" — omit entirely
- Do NOT list irrelevant technologies to pad Skills — UK AI hiring managers read fast and penalise padding
