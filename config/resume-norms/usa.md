# Resume Norms: USA Remote
Last updated: 2026-05-29
Source: EEOC compliance requirements, US tech recruiter conventions, remote contractor norms

## Mandatory inclusions

- **Work authorization statement**: REQUIRED. US companies must verify work authorization before any offer. For Amarsh's situation (India-based, not relocating, open to contractor):
  > "Not authorized to work in the US as a W-2 employee. Available as an independent contractor on a C2C (Corp-to-Corp) or 1099 basis for remote engagements."
  Place this as a note below the contact line OR in the last line of the Professional Summary.
- **"Remote" in contact/location line**: Make it explicit — "Location: India (Remote)" signals you are not expecting relocation.
- **LinkedIn URL**: US recruiters always check LinkedIn first.
- **GitHub URL**: Non-negotiable for AI/ML engineering roles in the US.
- **Portfolio / project links**: US tech market values demonstrable work. Include DevOps Copilot Plugin and PersonaRAG links.
- **Quantified impact**: Every bullet must have a number. US recruiters are trained to skip unquantified bullets.

## Prohibited / omit — EEOC compliance

These are legally sensitive in the US. Including them signals unfamiliarity with US hiring norms and can cause immediate rejection:

- **Photo**: NEVER. Discriminatory in US hiring context — automatic red flag.
- **Date of birth / age**: NEVER.
- **Nationality / citizenship country**: Do NOT include India nationality as a header field. Work auth statement (C2C/1099) handles this implicitly without naming nationality.
- **Marital status, religion, ethnicity**: NEVER.
- **"Visa required"**: Do NOT use this phrasing. Instead use the contractor framing above.

## Length

- **2 pages** for senior engineers (5+ years) is the US standard.
- 1 page is for students and early-career only.
- 3 pages is acceptable for 10+ year Staff/Principal level only.
- Amarsh: **2 pages**, Letter format.

## Photo

- **NEVER include.** See EEOC section above.

## Work authorization statement

This is the most important country-specific element for the USA.

**Standard phrasing for Amarsh:**

Option A (preferred for remote job boards):
> "Based in India. Available for remote engagements as an independent contractor (C2C / 1099). Not seeking US relocation or W-2 employment."

Option B (for companies that explicitly ask in forms):
> "Not authorized to work in the United States as a W-2 employee. Available as an independent contractor for remote roles."

**Why C2C framing works**: Many US startups and scale-ups hire international talent as contractors. Framing as "available as C2C contractor" is standard and removes the sponsorship conversation entirely.

**What to avoid**: Do NOT say "require H-1B sponsorship" — this is read as "very expensive and uncertain." The contractor framing sidesteps this entirely.

## Format

- **Paper**: Letter (8.5 × 11 inches) — NOT A4
- **Margins**: 0.5–0.75 inch
- **File format**: PDF for direct apply; DOCX sometimes requested by staffing agencies
- Section headers: standard US labels — "Professional Summary", "Work Experience", "Education", "Skills", "Projects"

## ATS / portals

- **LinkedIn**: Primary US job portal. Easy Apply frequently used.
- **Indeed US**: Large volume. ATS parses text from PDF.
- **Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby**: Used by most US startups and mid-size tech companies.
- **Workday**: Large US companies (Google, Meta, Amazon, banks). Paste into form fields AND upload PDF.
- **Wellfound (AngelList)**: US startup-focused. Contractor/remote roles common here.
- **Toptal / Gun.io**: Contractor marketplaces. Good fit for C2C positioning.
- **Arc.dev**: Remote-first tech talent platform. Explicitly supports international contractors.

## Summary framing

Lead with impact metrics, close with contractor availability. No nationality or relocation language:

```
Senior AI Engineer specialising in agentic systems, LLM platforms, and production GenAI.
6+ years building and deploying enterprise AI: AI Cockpit (multi-agent orchestration, 10x delivery acceleration),
LangGraph CI/CD migration agent (12x faster, hackathon winner at ~200-person competition), PersonaRAG
(eval-driven hybrid RAG, 37/37 pass rate across 4 eval suites). Deep expertise in RAG, MCP tooling,
LLMOps, and applied ML. Available for remote engagements as an independent contractor (C2C / 1099).
```

Key US market phrases to inject where authentic:
- "Staff-level impact", "0-to-1", "production-grade" — US seniority signals
- "cross-functional collaboration", "stakeholder alignment" — valued in US culture
- System design vocabulary: "distributed systems", "scalability", "low-latency inference"
- "open source contributions" — link DevOps Copilot Plugin prominently

## Red flags to avoid

- Do NOT include a photo under any circumstances
- Do NOT use A4 format — must be Letter
- Do NOT mention nationality, citizenship, or visa in the body of the resume (contractor statement handles it)
- Do NOT write "I" in first person — US resumes use third-person implied ("Built...", "Led...", not "I built...")
- Do NOT put a full mailing address — city/country is enough ("India (Remote)")
- Do NOT list salary expectations
- Do NOT use British spellings if writing in US English (colour → color, organised → organized)
